Questions

Before you choose a program

Answers to what people usually ask before enrolling.

Do I have to finish a program in order?+

Each program is a recommended sequence, but every course inside it also stands alone — start wherever your current skill level makes sense.

What's included in the hands-on labs?+

Real Windows, Linux, and cloud-console environments you practice in directly in the browser — not simulations or screenshots.

Is the Free plan enough to finish a program?+

Free covers IT Support training and networking basics. Premium unlocks all 10 programs, full lab access, and the complete video library.

Can I take individual courses without a program?+

Yes — every course in the Individual Courses section above is available on its own, with the same labs included.

Can my employer get bulk licenses?+

Yes — see the Business inquiries & bulk licensing section on the homepage, or contact sales@jobskillshare.org directly.
